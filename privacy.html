Privacy policy

updated november 23, 2026

BS2 (Ben Smith Business Solutions) is a one-person business in Ottawa, Ontario. This page explains what information I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. It follows Canada's private-sector privacy law (PIPEDA). I never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

I'm Ben Smith, and I'm personally responsible for how BS2 handles your information.

What I collect

  • When you book a call (Calendly): your name, email, the time you picked and your answers to the booking questions.
  • When you fill out the intake form on this website: your name, email, business details, what you need help with, your tools, budget, timeline and anything else you choose to share.
  • When you email me: whatever you include in the message.
  • If we work together: your billing contact details, quotes, agreements, invoices and payment records. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so I never see or store your full card number.

Please don't send me passwords, card numbers or other sensitive data through these forms. If I need access to one of your systems, we'll set it up securely together.

Why I collect it

  • To prepare for our call and understand your business.
  • To write quotes, deliver and maintain the work, and send invoices.
  • To keep my own business records, including which quotes became projects and why, so I can improve my services and pricing.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

By booking a call, filling out the form or emailing me, you agree to these uses. I only use your information for these purposes, and I won't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Your customers' data

When I build or maintain a system for a client, I may be able to see information about that client's own customers. I handle it only to do the work the client asked for, I don't use it for anything else, and I keep it confidential. The client stays in control of that data, and their own privacy policy applies to it.

Who else handles it

I use a few trusted services to run BS2. They store information on my behalf, and some of them store it outside Canada (mainly in the United States and the EU), where local laws apply:

  • Calendly for booking calls
  • Supabase to store intake answers and client records, and run the client portal
  • Google (Gmail, Calendar and Meet) for email, scheduling and video calls
  • Resend to send quotes and portal emails, and ImprovMX to forward email sent to bs2.ca
  • Stripe for payments
  • Anthropic (Claude) to help me turn my call notes into project details
  • GitHub Pages to host this website

I may also share information with my accountant, insurer or legal advisor when needed to run the business, or if the law requires it.

Cookies

This site doesn't use its own cookies or analytics. The booking calendar (Calendly) and the web fonts (Google Fonts) are loaded from their own services, which may collect basic technical data such as your IP address. See their privacy policies for details.

How long I keep it

  • Clients: project, invoice and payment records are kept for 6 years, as Canadian tax law requires.
  • Everyone else: booking and intake details are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Your customers' data: returned or deleted when our work together ends, unless you ask me to keep it.

You can ask me to delete your information sooner, unless I'm legally required to keep it.

Keeping it safe

I protect your information with password-protected accounts and two-factor authentication, and only I have access to it. If a breach ever creates a real risk of significant harm to you, I'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as possible, as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in Quebec

Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) gives you a few extra rights. You can ask me for a copy of your information in a common digital format, and ask how it's being used. I don't make automated decisions about you. Some of the services above store information outside Quebec, and I only use providers that protect it properly. If you're not satisfied with my answer, you can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
