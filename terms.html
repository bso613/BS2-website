These terms apply when you use this website or hire BS2 (Ben Smith Business Solutions), a sole proprietorship in Ottawa, Ontario. When we work together, we sign a services agreement and a quote for each project. Those replace this page for our work. This page is the short version.

Quotes and scope

  • Each quote lists the work, the price and the tools it uses. Quotes are valid for 30 days.
  • A quote is accepted when you sign it, accept it by email, or pay the deposit. Work starts once the deposit is paid, or once you set up a payment plan.

Fees and payment

  • Setup fee: 50% deposit to start, and the rest when the work is delivered or you start using it, whichever comes first. Or, if your quote says so, spread over 12 months (see "Setup payment plan" below).
  • Maintenance: billed monthly, in advance.
  • Tool costs (hosting, domains, software subscriptions): you pay these at cost, with no markup. Where possible, accounts are set up in your name and billed to you directly.

  • If an invoice is more than 30 days late, I may pause the work, including maintenance and any hosting I manage, after giving you 5 business days' notice.
  • Prices are in Canadian dollars. Sales tax is added where it applies.

Setup payment plan

If your quote offers it, you can pay the setup fee in monthly instalments instead of upfront. In that case:

  • How it works: the setup fee, plus any plan fee shown in your quote, is split into 12 equal monthly payments, added to your maintenance fee. No deposit is needed to start.
  • Minimum term: the plan has a 12-month minimum term, starting the month the work is delivered.
  • Automatic payments: payments are charged monthly to the card or bank account you set up through Stripe.
  • Price after 12 months: once the 12th payment is made, your monthly price drops to your maintenance fee alone. This happens automatically, with no new agreement needed.
  • Ending early: you can end the plan at any time with 30 days' written notice. The unpaid part of the setup fee then becomes due on your final invoice. You don't pay the maintenance fees for the months that remain.
  • Paying it off early: you can pay the remaining setup balance at any time, with no penalty.
